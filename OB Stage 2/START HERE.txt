OB STAGE 2 - ORDER BOOKING DASHBOARD
====================================

You have just extracted this folder. Two ways in.


JUST LOOK AT IT  (nothing to install)
-------------------------------------
Open this file in any browser:

    dashboard\Order_Booking_Dashboard_standalone.html

It is one self-contained file. Every filter and hover works. It shows the
data as of the last refresh and says SNAPSHOT at the top, because it is
reading a saved copy rather than your Excel.


RUN IT LIVE OFF EXCEL  (needs Python once)
------------------------------------------
1. Double-click              pipeline\run_service.cmd
2. Leave the black window open. Your browser opens at
   http://127.0.0.1:8787/  and the banner turns green: LIVE.

If Python is not installed, that window tells you exactly what to do and
stops - it will not fail silently. The one-line install, in PowerShell:

    winget install -e --id Python.Python.3.12

or from https://www.python.org/downloads/ - and tick
"Add python.exe to PATH" on the installer's FIRST screen.


ENTERING NEW DATA
-----------------
Open  Order_Booking_Master_STAGE2.xlsx
 -> sheet ASO_Raw -> click A2 -> paste values -> Ctrl+S -> F5 in the browser.

STOP PASTING AT COLUMN AA. Columns AB and AC hold the formulas that turn
the raw commission group and sales group into region and segment. Pasting
over them is the one action that breaks the workbook.

Full instructions, including what to do when an export would run past 500
rows:  docs\DAILY_USE.md
